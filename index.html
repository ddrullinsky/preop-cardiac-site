Cardiac Surgery Patient Companion

A calm companion for heart surgery

A free, bilingual (English/French) iPhone and iPad app for people having cardiac surgery — and the family beside them. Plain-language guides to common heart operations, preparation checklists, a recovery timeline, and clear guidance on when to get help. No accounts, no ads, fully offline.
